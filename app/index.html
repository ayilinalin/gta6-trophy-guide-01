Roadmap to platinum — about 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies

  1. Stage 1 — Story first. Play Chapters 1–5 and the side-character finales. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude before Chapter 1 ends — it is the only missable trophy — and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. There is no mission replay.
  2. Stage 2 — Collectibles. Cards, WAiNK locations, vehicles and garages, plus the two money trophies. Sweep region by region with the in-game trackers open.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities. Races, shooting ranges, drone races, robberies, stunts and challenges. Vaults and armored trucks unlock in Chapters 3–4.
  4. Stage 4 — Cleanup. Mop up the 100% checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion) for A Long Road; the platinum pops with it.

⚠ Only one trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — the Chapter 1 balcony meditation disappears after Chapter 1. Everything else stays available after the credits.
